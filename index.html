What the Real OSSLT Rubrics Reward

The real OSSLT opinion essay is scored on two rubrics: Topic Development (worth 60 percent of the essay's marks), which looks at whether you state a clear opinion, support it with reasons and details, and organize your paragraphs logically; and Conventions (worth 40 percent), which looks at your spelling, grammar, punctuation, and sentence structure. Compare your essay against the model above with both of those in mind, not just whether you agree with its opinion.
